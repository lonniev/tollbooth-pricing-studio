Delivery you can trust.

When a reply cannot reach the Secure Courier, Pricing Studio now says so
plainly instead of showing it as delivered. A message that reached some
relays but not the one the courier is listening on is marked amber — "Reply
did not reach the courier" — so you can send it again rather than waiting on
an approval that will never arrive.

Under the hood, the app now confirms a relay actually stored your message
before calling it sent.
